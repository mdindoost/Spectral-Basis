EXPERIMENTAL SETUP
------------------
X_label_label: ALL nodes → true one-hot label. Deterministic.
U_label_label: Rayleigh-Ritz of (L,D) on X_label_label.
span(X) = span(U) → all gaps are optimization geometry.

*** Scientific probe — test labels in features. NOT predictive. ***

6 models:
  GCN:         GCN_X | GCN_U (StandardScaler) | GCN_rowNorm_U (row-norm, Option B)
  Transformer: Transformer_X | Transformer_U | Transformer_rowNorm_U
  Transformer = 2-layer pre-norm TransformerEncoder, global self-attention,
  no graph adjacency, no positional encoding. hidden=256, heads=4, ff=512.
  Skipped for datasets with n > 25000 (O(N^2) infeasible).

Training:  SGD + Adam, LR∈{0.001,0.01,0.1}, 500 epochs, WD=0
Seeds:     5 train × 5 random split seeds = 25 runs per (model, opt, lr)
Canonical: adam, lr=0.01, all 5 split seeds pooled
