EXPERIMENTAL SETUP
------------------
X_label_label: ALL nodes → their true one-hot class label (deterministic).
U_label_label: Rayleigh-Ritz of (L,D) on X_label_label. Computed once per dataset.
span(X) = span(U) — gap between GCN(X) and GCN(U) is optimization geometry.

*** Scientific probe — test labels appear in features. NOT a predictive model. ***
GCN(X_label_label) = transductive label propagation via message passing.
Upper bound: measures ceiling performance + geometry of spectral projection.

3 models:  GCN_X | GCN_U (StandardScaler) | GCN_rowNorm_U (row-norm at layer input)
Training:  SGD + Adam, LR∈{0.001,0.01,0.1}, 500 epochs, WD=0
Seeds:     5 train × 5 random split seeds (60/20/20) = 25 runs per (opt,lr,model)
Canonical: adam, lr=0.01, all 5 split seeds pooled
