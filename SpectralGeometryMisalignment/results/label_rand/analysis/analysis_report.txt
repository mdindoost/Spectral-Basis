EXPERIMENTAL SETUP
------------------
X_label_rand: train nodes → one-hot class label, val/test → N(0,1).
U_label_rand: Rayleigh-Ritz of (L,D) on X_label_rand. d_eff ≤ num_classes.
span(X) = span(U) — gap between GCN(X) and GCN(U) is optimization geometry.

3 models:  GCN_X | GCN_U (StandardScaler) | GCN_rowNorm_U (row-norm at layer input)
Training:  SGD + Adam, LR∈{0.001,0.01,0.1}, 500 epochs, WD=0
Seeds:     5 train × 3 xrand draws × 6 split configs (1 fixed + 5 random)
Canonical: adam, lr=0.01, random split, split_seed=0
